          About

          What to Wear shows an illustrated outfit for the weather at a US location, today or on a trip. Created by Neela Farzin.

          Data sources

          • Weather: National Weather Service (api.weather.gov). Forecasts cover about 7 days. The "now" temperature is the forecast for the current hour, not a station reading.
          • Place search: Nominatim, using data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Searches are limited to the United States.

          How recommendations are made

          • Outfit by the day’s forecast high: below 40°F very cold, 40–59 chilly, 60–80 neutral, 81–90 hot, 91 and up very hot. There is no official standard for temperature-to-clothing; these cutoffs are our judgment, informed by consumer dressing guides such as Fit The Forecast.
          • Umbrella: shown when the National Weather Service chance of precipitation is 40% or higher. NWS defines that chance as the probability of at least 0.01 inch of rain (NWS forecast terms); the 40% cutoff is our choice, since NWS sets no umbrella threshold.
          • Extra water: shown when the NWS heat index reaches 85°F or higher, in line with NOAA heat index guidance on heat-related fatigue and the need to drink more.
          • Outfit and reminder wording are picked at random from a few options and saved, so a date looks the same when you return. The arrows beside the character choose a different outfit.

          Privacy

          • Everything stays in your browser. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking.
          • Your browser stores your latest Home location, saved trips, the outfit and wording picked for each date, and your settings (character, colors, auto-delete). The °F/°C choice is not saved.
          • Place names you search for, and coordinates, are sent only to Nominatim and api.weather.gov to get a place and a forecast.
          • Your device location is requested only when you press “Use my location.” If you decline, you can type a place instead.
